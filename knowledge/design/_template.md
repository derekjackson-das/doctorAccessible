# Design: Topic

Design judgment in two clearly separated parts: the **Requirement**, the WCAG 2.2 Level AA minimum, cited to the `wcag/` entries (tier 1); and the **Practice** above it, cited to W3C informative material and Level AAA criteria (beyond our target) or to named practitioners (tier 2 or 3), and marked as practice, not requirement.

- Requirement sources (tier 1): `wcag/...` entries.
- Practice sources: W3C Understanding pages or AAA criteria (tier 2, or tier 1 beyond target); practitioners from `knowledge/SOURCES.md` (tier 3). Author, "Title", date and updated date, URL.
- Related entries: `testing/...`, `patterns/...`, `coga/...`
- Verified against the live sources: YYYY-MM-DD by <name>

## Status and reporting

A design that fails the Requirement is reported under its criterion, once, at the confidence its `wcag/` entry gives. A design that meets the Requirement but misses a Practice point is reported as likely or manual review, never definite, and never as a WCAG failure. Level AAA criteria are practice for this project.

## Requirement (tier 1)

What the AA criteria ask, in our words, with the entry for each. No numbers beyond what the entries state.

## Practice (tier 2 and 3, not required)

Each point marked with its source and tier, and why it goes beyond the minimum.

## How to check

The measurements and checks that separate a pass on paper from a pass in use. Link `testing/` entries for the manual procedure.
