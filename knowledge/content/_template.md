# Content: Topic

The why behind one of the house standards in `docs/content-standards.md`, with how to apply and check it. The standard is the rule; this entry is its reasoning and method.

- House standard: `docs/content-standards.md`, section n.
- Sources: author or organization, "Title", date (or "no date shown", accessed date), URL, tier. From `knowledge/SOURCES.md` only.
- WCAG: `wcag/...` entries where a criterion touches the topic, or "no entry yet".
- Verified against the live sources: YYYY-MM-DD by <name>

## Status and reporting

A text that fails a WCAG criterion is reported under that criterion, once, at the confidence its `wcag/` entry gives. A text that meets WCAG but misses the house standard or the sources' guidance is reported as likely or manual review, never definite, and never as a WCAG failure. A project's `a11y-spec.md` may change the standard; the spec wins.
